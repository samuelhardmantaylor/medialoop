Resources

Tools that Dr. Taylor and his collaborators have created, free to use. If you have questions about how to use them, email Sam.

Scale01 / 05PARISPerceived Algorithm Responsiveness and Insensitivity Scale. This 15-item scale measures two variables: perceived algorithm responsiveness (PAR) and perceived algorithm insensitivity (PAI). It is validated for Facebook, Twitter, TikTok and Instagram; more information is in our Social Media + Society publication.Get the scale Open materials02 / 05Open Science FrameworkFor many studies you can find the preregistration, survey or experiment materials, data and code on the Open Science Framework. If you are looking for a specific scale or procedure, start on OSF.Go to OSF Experiment platform03 / 05TrumanA social media simulation: a fake social networking site where participants are randomly assigned to conditions when they sign up, and interact with pre-programmed bots the researcher controls. Everything needed to replicate our experiments or run your own is on GitHub. Using Truman takes technical skills, especially JavaScript; more is in our CHI and CSCW papers.View on GitHub Talks04 / 05Presentations and postersA folder of slides from past conference presentations, colloquiums and job talks, plus a few academic posters.Open the folder Papers05 / 05Research publicationsPre-print PDFs of some harder-to-find and in-press articles, such as book chapters.
